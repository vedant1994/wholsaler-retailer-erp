Frequently Asked Questions

What is an E-Invoice with QR Code verification?
An E-Invoice is a digitally structured bill containing a unique QR code. When scanned by any smartphone camera or barcode reader, the QR code decodes essential invoice metadata (invoice number, store GSTIN, date, and total amount) for instant verification.
What is the difference between a Wholesaler and Shopkeeper profile?
Wholesalers manage networks of retail shops and issue B2B supply invoices. Shopkeepers manage retail stores, catalog products, and generate customer checkout invoices.
Is my commercial billing data secure?
Yes. All stored data is protected by Django’s security framework, including CSRF tokens, SQL injection prevention, and password encryption.
